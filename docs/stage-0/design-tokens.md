# SAPIEN Design Token Plan

## Source of truth

The official uploaded SAPIEN logo is the only design source of truth for the visual system. The visual language must be derived from the logo and its actual theme.

## Design principles

- premium
- minimal
- calm
- intelligent
- precise
- distinctively SAPIEN
- clear hierarchy
- whitespace-led layout
- disciplined reduction
- no trend-driven decoration

## Core brand observation from the logo

The uploaded mark uses:

- a dark navy dominating the silhouette
- a warm gold ribbon accent
- a softened white and neutral background
- strong geometric restraint
- a confident, institutional, future-skills character

### 2. Semantic usage rules

- Navy is the institutional foundation.
- Gold is used sparingly and purposefully for authority, emphasis, and active actions.
- White is used for high-contrast reading on navy surfaces.
- Neutral tones support surfaces while maintaining calmness.
- No additional color families are introduced unless a later approved design requirement explicitly demands it.

### 3. Typography

Use type with strong institutional clarity. Based on the logo and modern premium academic branding, the design direction prefers:

- bold geometric sans for headings and institutional naming
- clear, readable sans for body text
- high legibility over decorative expression
- strong hierarchy rather than excessive weight variation

Recommended system approach:

- `font.display`: strong condensed / geometric sans style
- `font.body`: clean modern sans with strong readability
- `font.mono`: technical and data-oriented for IDs, code, metrics, and ledger items

### 4. Layout and spacing

- generous whitespace
- disciplined alignment grids
- minimal panel clutter
- strong separation between functions
- not every block must be a card; use moderation

Recommended spacing cadence:

- `space.1`: 4px
- `space.2`: 8px
- `space.3`: 12px
- `space.4`: 16px
- `space.5`: 20px
- `space.6`: 24px
- `space.8`: 32px
- `space.10`: 40px
- `space.12`: 48px

### 5. Visual treatment rules

- Keep surfaces mostly dark navy and charcoal with gold highlights.
- Use gold primarily for calls to action and status authority.
- Do not create generic AI-dashboard patterns.
- Do not add excessive gradients, random silhouettes, or decorative clutter.
- Do not add more than the approved palette and its purposeful derivatives.
- Do not reinterpret the logo with alternate colors or forms.

### 6. Only approved logo usage rules

- Use the official logo file, not a redraw.
- Do not distort, stretch, rotate, or recolor the mark.
- No competing identity is allowed.
- The logo must be used consistently on web, ERP, student portal, receipts, documents, and forms.
